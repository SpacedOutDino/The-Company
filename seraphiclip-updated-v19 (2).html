String dial

Drag a point or click the string to add one. Up = faster / louder. The dotted line is normal: 1× speed or 100% of clip volume. The horizontal axis follows source time; shaded sections are trimmed out. Curves save automatically and apply to playback and export. Speed changes also change audio pitch.
